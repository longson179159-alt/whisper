
Lesson 2: Find Your First Lesson

Now, let's find your first lesson in Langoda!

Langoda has many lessons for different English levels. Some lessons are easy, and others are more difficult.

If you are new to English, start with an A1 lesson. A1 lessons use simple words and short sentences.

You can explore different courses and choose a topic you like. Learning is more fun when you read about something interesting!

Click on a lesson to open it. You can read the text and listen to the audio.

Do not worry if you see some new words. You will learn them as you read.

Now, find an A1 lesson and start reading!
