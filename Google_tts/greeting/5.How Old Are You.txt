Lesson 5: How Old Are You?

Anna: How old are you, Tom?

Tom: I'm twenty-five years old. How old are you?

Anna: I'm twenty-four.

Tom: When is your birthday?

Anna: My birthday is in May. What about you?

Tom: My birthday is in August.

Anna: Nice! Are you twenty-six next year?

Tom: Yes, I am.

Anna: Happy early birthday!

Tom: Thank you, Anna!
