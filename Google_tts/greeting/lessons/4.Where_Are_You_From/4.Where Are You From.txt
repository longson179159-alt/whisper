Lesson 4: Where Are You From?

Anna: Where are you from, Tom?

Tom: I'm from the United States. What about you?

Anna: I'm from Canada.

Tom: Oh, nice! Which city are you from?

Anna: I'm from Toronto. Do you know Toronto?

Tom: Yes, I do! It's a big city.

Anna: That's right! Where do you live now?

Tom: I live in New York. How about you?

Anna: I live in New York, too!

Tom: Really? That's great!
