
Lesson 7: What Do You Like to Do?

Anna: What do you like to do in your free time, Tom?

Tom: I like reading books and playing basketball.

Anna: Do you play basketball every day?

Tom: No, only on weekends. What about you?

Anna: I like listening to music and watching movies.

Tom: What kind of movies do you like?

Anna: I love funny movies!

Tom: Me too! They're always fun to watch!
