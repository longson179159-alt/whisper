
Lesson 3: What's Your Name?

Anna: Hi! What's your name?

Tom: My name is Tom. What's your name?

Anna: I'm Anna. Nice to meet you, Tom!

Tom: Nice to meet you, too!

Anna: Is Tom your first name?

Tom: Yes, it is. My full name is Thomas Brown.

Anna: Can I call you Tom?

Tom: Of course!

Anna: Great! You can call me Anna.

Tom: Sounds good!
