Lesson 2: How Are You?
Anna: Hi, Tom! How are you?

Tom: I'm good, thanks. How about you?

Anna: I'm fine, thank you!

Tom: That's good!

Anna: How are you feeling today?

Tom: I'm a little tired.

Anna: Oh, why?

Tom: I didn't sleep well last night.

Anna: I'm sorry to hear that. Take care of yourself!
