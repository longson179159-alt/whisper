Lesson 6: Are You a Student?

Anna: Are you a student, Tom?

Tom: Yes, I am. I study English at college.

Anna: That's interesting! Do you like it?

Tom: Yes, I do. What about you? Are you a student?

Anna: No, I'm not. I'm a teacher.

Tom: Really? What do you teach?

Anna: I teach English at a small school.

Tom: That's great! Do you like your job?

Anna: Yes, I love it!
