Lesson 13: I'll Help You

Grammar: Will — Offers and Spontaneous Decisions | Level: A1

Jack: Hi, Emma! I have a problem.

Emma: What's wrong, Jack?

Jack: My computer isn't working. I need to finish my homework.

Emma: Don't worry! I'll help you.

Jack: Really? Thank you!

Emma: I'll bring my laptop to your apartment.

Jack: That's great! I'll make some tea for us.

Emma: Sounds good! Oh, it's raining outside.

Jack: I'll get an umbrella for you.

Emma: Thanks, Jack! I'll see you in ten minutes.

Jack: See you soon!
