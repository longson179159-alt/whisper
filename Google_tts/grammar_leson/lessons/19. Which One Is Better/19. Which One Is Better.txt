Lesson 19: Which One Is Better?

Grammar: Comparatives and Superlatives | Level: A1

Emma: Hi, Jack! I want to buy a new phone.

Jack: Nice! Which one do you like?

Emma: I like these two phones. Which one is better?

Jack: The black phone is cheaper than the white one.

Emma: That's true, but the white phone has a bigger screen.

Jack: Yes, and it has a better camera too.

Emma: Which one has the best battery?

Jack: The black one. It has the longest battery life.

Emma: Hmm, I think the black phone is the best choice for me.

Jack: Good choice! It's cheaper and has a great battery.
