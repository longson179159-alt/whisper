Lesson 15: You Should Get Some Rest

Grammar: Should / Have To / Must | Level: A1

Emma: Hi, Jack! You look tired. Are you okay?

Jack: I'm really tired. I didn't sleep well last night.

Emma: You should get some rest.

Jack: I know, but I have to finish my work today.

Emma: You must take care of yourself, Jack!

Jack: You're right. What should I do?

Emma: You should drink some water and go to bed early.

Jack: Good idea! I must finish my work first.

Emma: And you shouldn't drink coffee too late.

Jack: Thanks, Emma! I'll try to sleep early tonight.
