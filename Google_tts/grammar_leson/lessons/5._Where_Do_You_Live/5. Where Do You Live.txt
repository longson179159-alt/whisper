
Lesson 5: Where Do You Live?

Grammar: Wh- Questions — What / Where / When / Who / How | Level: A1

Emma: Hi, Jack! Where do you live?

Jack: I live on Green Street, near the park.

Emma: Nice! Who do you live with?

Jack: I live with my brother.

Emma: What's his name?

Jack: His name is Mike.

Emma: What does he do?

Jack: He works at a hospital.

Emma: Interesting! When does he start work?

Jack: He starts at eight in the morning.

Emma: How does he get to work?

Jack: He takes the bus every day.
