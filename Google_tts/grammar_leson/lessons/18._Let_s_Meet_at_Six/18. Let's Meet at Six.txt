Lesson 18: Let's Meet at Six

Grammar: Prepositions — In / On / At / To / For | Level: A1

Emma: Hi, Jack! Are you free on Saturday?

Jack: Yes, I am! What do you want to do?

Emma: Let's go to the movies.

Jack: Good idea! What time?

Emma: Let's meet at six in the evening.

Jack: Where should we meet?

Emma: At the café next to the movie theater.

Jack: Great! We can have coffee before the movie.

Emma: Perfect! I'll buy the tickets for us.

Jack: Thanks, Emma! See you on Saturday!

Emma: See you at six!
