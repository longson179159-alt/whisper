Lesson 14: Ordering Lunch

Grammar: Would Like / Want To | Level: A1

Emma: Hi, Jack! What would you like to eat?

Jack: I'd like a chicken sandwich. What about you?

Emma: I think I'd like some pasta.

Jack: Sounds good! Do you want to order a drink?

Emma: Yes, I'd like some orange juice.

Jack: I'll have a glass of water.

Emma: Do you want to order something sweet too?

Jack: Yes! I'd like a piece of chocolate cake.

Emma: That sounds delicious! I want to try it too.

Jack: Great! Let's order one piece and share it.

Emma: Good idea!
