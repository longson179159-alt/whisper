Lesson 9: What Are You Doing?

Grammar: Present Continuous — am / is / are + -ing | Level: A1

Emma: Hi, Jack! What are you doing?

Jack: Hey, Emma! I'm watching TV. What about you?

Emma: I'm cooking dinner.

Jack: Nice! What are you making?

Emma: I'm making chicken soup.

Jack: Sounds delicious! Is your brother helping you?

Emma: No, he isn't. He's playing video games.

Jack: That's funny! My brother is studying in his room.

Emma: What are your parents doing?

Jack: They're watching a movie.

Emma: Sounds like a relaxing evening!

Jack: Yes, it is!
