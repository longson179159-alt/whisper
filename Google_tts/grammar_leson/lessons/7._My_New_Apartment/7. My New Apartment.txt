Lesson 7: My New Apartment

Grammar: There Is / There Are | Level: A1

Emma: Hi, Jack! How's your new apartment?

Jack: It's great! There is a big living room and a small kitchen.

Emma: Nice! Is there a sofa in the living room?

Jack: Yes, there is. There are also two chairs and a table.

Emma: Are there any pictures on the walls?

Jack: No, there aren't. But there is a big window.

Emma: What about your bedroom?

Jack: There is a bed and a desk. There are also some books on the desk.

Emma: Sounds like a lovely apartment!
