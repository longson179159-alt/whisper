Lesson 2: My Daily Routine

Grammar: Present Simple — Affirmative | Level: A1

Emma: Hi, Jack! What do you do every morning?

Jack: I wake up at seven. Then I eat breakfast and drink coffee.

Emma: What time do you start work?

Jack: I start work at nine. I work in an office.

Emma: That sounds nice! I work at a school.

Jack: Really? What do you teach?

Emma: I teach English. My students study hard every day.

Jack: That's great! You have an interesting job.

Emma: Thanks, Jack! I really enjoy my work.
