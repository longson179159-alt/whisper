Lesson 10: My Weekend

Grammar: Past Simple — Regular and Irregular Verbs | Level: A1

Emma: Hi, Jack! How was your weekend?

Jack: It was great! I visited my parents on Saturday.

Emma: Nice! What did you do there?

Jack: We cooked dinner and watched a movie together. What about you?

Emma: Sounds fun! I went to the beach with my friends.

Jack: Really? Did you swim?

Emma: Yes, I did! We swam and played volleyball.

Jack: That's great! What did you eat?

Emma: We ate pizza and drank orange juice.

Jack: Sounds like a wonderful weekend!

Emma: Yes, I really enjoyed it!
