Lesson 8: Can You Swim?

Grammar: Can / Can't | Level: A1

Emma: Hey, Jack! Can you swim?

Jack: Yes, I can! I swim every weekend.

Emma: That's great! I can't swim very well.

Jack: Don't worry! I can teach you.

Emma: Thanks! Can you drive a car too?

Jack: Yes, I can. But I can't ride a motorcycle.

Emma: That's funny! I can ride a motorcycle, but I can't drive a car.

Jack: What about cooking? Can you cook?

Emma: Yes, I can! I can make pizza and pasta.

Jack: Nice! I can't cook very well. Maybe you can teach me!

Emma: Of course!
