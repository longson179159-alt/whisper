Lesson 17: Is This Your Bag?
Grammar: Pronouns and Possessives | Level: A1

Emma: Hey, Jack! Is this your bag?

Jack: No, it isn't mine. My bag is black.

Emma: Oh, this one is blue. Wait, it's mine!

Jack: Great! Is this your phone too?

Emma: No, it isn't. My phone is white.

Jack: Oh, this one is mine. Thanks!

Emma: And whose jacket is this?

Jack: It's my brother's. His jacket is blue.

Emma: What about this red jacket?

Jack: That's my sister's. It's hers.

Emma: Great! Now we have everything!
