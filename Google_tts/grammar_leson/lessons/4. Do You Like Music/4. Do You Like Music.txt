Lesson 4: Do You Like Music?

Grammar: Yes/No Questions — Do / Does | Level: A1

Emma: Hey, Jack! Do you like music?

Jack: Yes, I do! I listen to music every day.

Emma: Do you play any instruments?

Jack: Yes, I do. I play the guitar.

Emma: That's cool! Does your brother play the guitar too?

Jack: No, he doesn't. He plays the piano.

Emma: Nice! Do you play together?

Jack: Sometimes! What about you? Do you play any instruments?

Emma: No, I don't. But I love singing!

Jack: Really? Does your sister like singing too?

Emma: Yes, she does! We sing together on weekends.
