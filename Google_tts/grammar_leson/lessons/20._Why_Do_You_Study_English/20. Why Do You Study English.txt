Lesson 20: Why Do You Study English?

Grammar: Connectors — And / But / Because / So / If | Level: A1

Emma: Hi, Jack! Why do you study English?

Jack: I study English because I want to travel and meet new people.

Emma: That's great! I study English because I love reading books.

Jack: Is English difficult for you?

Emma: Sometimes! I know many words, but speaking is still hard.

Jack: Me too! I practice every day, so I can improve.

Emma: What will you do if your English gets better?

Jack: If my English gets better, I'll travel to America.

Emma: That sounds exciting!

Jack: Yes! Learning English takes time, but it's worth it.
