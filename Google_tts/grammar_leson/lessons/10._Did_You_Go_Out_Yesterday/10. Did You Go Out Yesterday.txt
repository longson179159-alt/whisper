Lesson 11: Did You Go Out Yesterday?

Grammar: Past Simple — Questions and Negatives | Level: A1

Emma: Hi, Jack! Did you go out yesterday?

Jack: No, I didn't. I stayed home all day.

Emma: Really? Why didn't you go out?

Jack: Because it rained all day.

Emma: Did you watch TV?

Jack: No, I didn't. I read a book instead.

Emma: That sounds nice! Did your brother stay home too?

Jack: No, he didn't. He went to work.

Emma: Did you cook dinner?

Jack: No, I didn't cook. I ordered pizza.

Emma: Sounds like a relaxing day!

Jack: Yes, it was!
