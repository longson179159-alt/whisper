Lesson 12: Plans for Saturday

Grammar: Be Going To — Future Plans | Level: A1

Emma: Hi, Jack! What are you going to do this Saturday?

Jack: I'm going to visit my grandparents. What about you?

Emma: I'm going to go shopping with my sister.

Jack: Nice! What are you going to buy?

Emma: I'm going to buy some new clothes.

Jack: Sounds fun! Are you going to have lunch together?

Emma: Yes, we are. We're going to eat at a restaurant.

Jack: That's great! I'm going to have dinner with my grandparents.

Emma: Sounds like a nice weekend!

Jack: Yes! I'm really looking forward to it.
