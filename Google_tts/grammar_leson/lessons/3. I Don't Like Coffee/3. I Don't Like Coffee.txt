Lesson 3: I Don't Like Coffee

Grammar: Don't / Doesn't | Level: A1

Emma: Hi, Jack! Do you want some coffee?

Jack: No, thanks. I don't like coffee.

Emma: Really? What do you drink in the morning?

Jack: I usually drink tea. I don't drink coffee because it's too bitter.

Emma: That's interesting! My brother doesn't like coffee either.

Jack: What about you?

Emma: I love coffee, but I don't like tea.

Jack: That's funny! We don't like the same drinks.

Emma: Well, we both like orange juice!

Jack: That's true!
