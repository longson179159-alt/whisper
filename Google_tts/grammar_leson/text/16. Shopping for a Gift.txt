Lesson 16: Shopping for a Gift

Grammar: Articles — A / An / The | Level: A1

Emma: Hi, Jack! What are you looking for?

Jack: I'm looking for a birthday gift for my sister.

Emma: How about a book?

Jack: That's a good idea! She loves reading.

Emma: Look! There's an interesting book over there.

Jack: Oh, the book looks nice! How much is it?

Emma: It's ten dollars.

Jack: Great! I'll buy the book.

Emma: Do you want to buy a birthday card too?

Jack: Yes! There's a beautiful card next to the books.

Emma: Perfect! Your sister will love the gifts!
