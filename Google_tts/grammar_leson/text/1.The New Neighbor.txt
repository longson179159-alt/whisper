Lesson 1: The New Neighbor

Grammar: To Be — am / is / are | Level: A1

Emma: Hi! I'm Emma. Are you new here?

Jack: Yes, I am. I'm Jack.

Emma: Nice to meet you, Jack! Where are you from?

Jack: I'm from Boston. Are you from here?

Emma: Yes, I am. I'm your neighbor!

Jack: Oh, that's great! Is this a quiet neighborhood?

Emma: Yes, it is. The people here are very friendly.

Jack: That's good to hear. Nice to meet you, Emma!

Emma: Nice to meet you too, Jack!
