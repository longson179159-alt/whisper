Lesson 6: Do You Have a Pet?

Grammar: Have / Has | Level: A1

Emma: Hi, Jack! Do you have any pets?

Jack: Yes, I have a dog. His name is Max.

Emma: That's cute! How old is he?

Jack: He's two years old. He has big ears and brown eyes.

Emma: Nice! I have a cat. Her name is Lucy.

Jack: Does your cat have long hair?

Emma: Yes, she does! She has long white hair.

Jack: Does your brother have any pets?

Emma: No, he doesn't. But he loves animals!

Jack: That's great! Pets are wonderful friends.
