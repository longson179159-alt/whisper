James goes to a large supermarket near his home every Saturday morning.

He likes shopping there because the supermarket is clean, bright, and easy to walk around.

There are many fresh apples, bananas, tomatoes, and other vegetables at good prices.

Next, James walks to the meat and dairy sections, where he can easily find everything on his shopping list.

He also likes the bakery because the bread is fresh and smells wonderful.

The supermarket has many different products, so James does not need to visit several smaller stores.
