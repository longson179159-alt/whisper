On Friday evening, Tom opens the box and counts his money.

He thinks there should be sixty dollars, but there are only forty dollars in the box.

Tom is surprised and counts the money again, but the number is still the same.

He looks under his bed and inside his desk because he sometimes leaves money there.

Then he asks his sister if she saw any money in his room, but she says no.

Tom tries to remember if he bought something during the week.

He remembers buying lunch on Tuesday, but he paid for it with money from his wallet.
