He usually gets up at seven o'clock and drinks a glass of water.

For breakfast, he eats eggs and fruit instead of sweet food.

He walks to school when the weather is nice.

After school, he sometimes plays basketball with his friends.

In the evening, he helps his mother prepare vegetables for dinner.
